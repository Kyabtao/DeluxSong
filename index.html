Welcome to

A Neighbourhood Saloon, Rebuilt in Sound

Deluxe Saloon is a free ambient radio built to recreate one very specific memory: the neighbourhood hair-cutting saloon of 2000s India, with a battered radio in the corner playing Bollywood hits between the buzz of clippers and the snip of scissors. Press play, and it streams a nonstop mix of 2000s Bollywood radio nostalgia, styled around the sights, sounds and one-liners of a classic Indian barbershop.
