Good to know

How the Loop Works

🔀

Shuffle by Default

By default the radio shuffles these twelve tracks forever, so no song repeats until the loop does. You can switch to in-order play on the Radio.

📺

Streams from YouTube

Every track streams straight from YouTube — nothing is downloaded, nothing is stored. The same playlist lives on the Ishtar Music channel.

🎼

Music Belongs to Its Owners

All songs are credited to their labels and the Ishtar Music uploads. This is a demonstration build; rights stay with the respective owners.
