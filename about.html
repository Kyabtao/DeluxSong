The Story

About Deluxe Saloon

One very specific memory, rebuilt: the neighbourhood hair-cutting saloon of 2000s India, with a battered radio in the corner playing Bollywood hits between the buzz of clippers and the snip of scissors.

Why this exists

Deluxe Saloon (also spelled Deluxe Salon or Delux Salon) started as a question: what if you could step back into that saloon just by pressing play? The answer is this site — a free ambient radio that streams a nonstop mix of 2000s Bollywood radio nostalgia, styled around the sights, sounds and one-liners of a classic Indian barbershop.

No sign-up. No app store. No ads. Just the radio, the clippers, the chai, and the barbers quoting movies at you.

Under the hood

How it works

  • Radio — every track streams through the YouTube IFrame player. Nothing is downloaded; your connection goes straight to YouTube.
  • Baarish — no audio files at all. The rain is brown-ish noise shaped by low-pass and high-pass filters, with a 55 Hz sine for the distant thunder. It all happens on your device.
  • Multi-page, mobile-first — Home, Radio, Tracks, About, FAQ and Support are real pages with a thumb-sized nav, and a little player bar at the bottom keeps your song with you as you move around.
  • Installable — a web manifest plus a service worker let you pin the saloon to your home screen; it opens like an app.
  • Live chat — when the site is served by its Node server (npm start), a WebSocket room runs at /ws: 200-message history, online counter, rate limiting. On static hosting the chat simply shows as offline and everything else keeps working.

The fine print

Fair use, fair credit
