    Deluxe Saloon — a 2000s Indian Saloon, Rebuilt in Sound

    Deluxe Saloon (also spelled Deluxe Salon or Delux Salon) is a free ambient radio built to recreate one very specific memory: the neighbourhood hair-cutting saloon of 2000s India, with a battered radio in the corner playing Bollywood hits between the buzz of clippers and the snip of scissors. Press play, and Deluxe Saloon streams a nonstop mix of 2000s Bollywood radio nostalgia, styled around the sights, sounds and one-liners of a classic Indian barbershop.
