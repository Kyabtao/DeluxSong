About TCS Radio

Retro songs for modern chai breaks.

TCS Radio is a free, fan-made radio by Umair — 2000s Bollywood nostalgia for the office, the auto, the highway, and the chai tapri. No account needed.

  • Free & ad-free, forever — 17 fan-made stations: Office, Auto, Truck, Monsoon, Chai Tapri, Indipop, Latest Hits, Love Songs, singer specials, New Songs and Golden Oldies.
  • Pick a station or song — tap ☰ on the player deck to open the playlist sidebar; the music and artwork switch right away.
  • It starts on its own — the radio plays as soon as the page opens; if the browser holds back sound, one tap anywhere turns the volume on.
  • Nonstop flow — shuffle, skip, seek and volume stay on the deck, and when one station ends the next starts automatically.
  • Install & keep listening — use Add to Home Screen and playback continues in the background or on the lock screen.
  • Suggest or remove a song — hit 🎵 Add / Remove Songs below; requests go straight to Umair and removals are reviewed promptly.
  • The extras — 🌧️ Baarish adds optional rain sound & on-screen rain; 🛡️ AdBlock only hides and skips ads inside your own browser, which is 100% legal.
