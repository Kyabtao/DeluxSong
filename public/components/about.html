TCS Radio — 2000s India Rebuilt in Ambient Sound

TCS Radio is a free retro ambient radio created by Umair to capture the unforgettable sounds, humor, and music of 2000s India. Whether you're coding in an IT tech park cubicle, zipping through city traffic in an auto rickshaw, or cruising down the GT Road with dhaba trucks, TCS Radio brings the golden era of Bollywood right to your browser with zero sign-up.

🏢

Office (TCS Tech Park)

Soulful 2000s Bollywood staples by KK, Mohit Chauhan, Lucky Ali, and A.R. Rahman — perfect for debugging, timesheet filling, and chai breaks.

🛺

Auto Rickshaw (Jhankar)

High-octane 90s/2000s Jhankar beats with heavy bass, cassette nostalgia, and iconic tracks that rule Indian city streets.

🚚

Highway Truck (Dhaba Beats)

Loud, celebratory Punjabi and Bollywood highway anthems with 'Horn OK Please' energy, dhol rhythms, and road trip vibes.

Monsoon & Chai Tapri

Romantic late-night rain melodies and roadside tea-stall classics that take you straight back to the 90s cassette player days.
